## Summary of Situation

The International Court of Justice (ICJ) issued provisional measures in January 2024 finding it plausible that Israel is committing genocide in Gaza (Case: South Africa v. Israel). As of mid-2026, over 45,000 Palestinian civilians have been killed (UN figures), with over 100,000 injured. The UN Special Rapporteur on Palestine declared a genocide is taking place.

Multiple UN agencies, Amnesty International, and Human Rights Watch have documented war crimes, collective punishment, starvation as a weapon of war, and the targeting of civilian infrastructure (hospitals, schools, refugee camps).
