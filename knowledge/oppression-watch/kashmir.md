## Summary of Situation

Indian-administered Jammu & Kashmir has been under military occupation for decades. After Article 370 was revoked in August 2019, a complete communications blackout (internet, phone) lasting over a year was imposed — the longest internet shutdown in a democracy in history. Human Rights Watch, Amnesty International, and the UN High Commissioner for Human Rights have documented:

- Extrajudicial killings and enforced disappearances
- Mass arbitrary detention (including of minors)
- Torture in custody
- Restrictions on press freedom and public assembly
- Pellet guns used against civilian protesters causing mass blindings

Kashmir is disputed territory; Pakistan, India, and Kashmiris themselves have competing claims. The UN has passed resolutions calling for a plebiscite that has never been held.
