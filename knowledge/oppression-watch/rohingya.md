# Oppression Watch: Rohingya Muslims (Myanmar)

**Last updated:** 2026-06-16
**Next scheduled update:** 2026-06-18
**Status:** Ongoing — genocide declared; 900,000+ refugees; Myanmar military coup 2021

---

## Summary of Situation

The International Court of Justice (ICJ) is hearing The Gambia v. Myanmar genocide case (filed 2019). The UN Fact-Finding Mission on Myanmar (2018) concluded that the Myanmar military committed genocide, crimes against humanity, and war crimes against Rohingya Muslims. The 2017 military crackdown drove 700,000+ Rohingya into Bangladesh in what UN officials called "textbook ethnic cleansing."

After the military coup (February 2021), Myanmar's military (Tatmadaw) continues to commit atrocities against Rohingya, Karen, and other minority groups. International businesses that funded the military during this period have faced significant scrutiny.
