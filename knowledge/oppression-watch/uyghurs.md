# Oppression Watch: Uyghur Muslims (Xinjiang, China)

**Last updated:** 2026-06-16
**Next scheduled update:** 2026-06-18
**Status:** Ongoing — mass detention, cultural genocide, forced labor, surveillance

---

## Summary of Situation

The Chinese government has detained an estimated 1–1.8 million Uyghur Muslims in "re-education camps" in Xinjiang since 2017. The UN Human Rights Office (August 2022) found evidence of "serious human rights violations" that "may constitute international crimes, in particular crimes against humanity." Multiple governments (US, UK, Canada, Netherlands, Belgium) have officially declared the treatment of Uyghurs a genocide.

Documented atrocities: forced labor in factories, forced sterilization, destruction of mosques, banning of Islamic practices (prayer, fasting, hijab), mass surveillance, family separation.
