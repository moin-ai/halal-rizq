# Islamic Ruling: Riba (Interest) & Conventional Banking

**Last reviewed:** 2026-06-16
**Consensus level:** Near-unanimous — one of the clearest prohibitions in Islam

---

## Quranic Foundation

> "Allah has permitted trade and forbidden riba." — Quran 2:275
> "O you who believe! Fear Allah and give up what remains of your demand for riba, if you are indeed believers." — Quran 2:278
> "If you do not, then take notice of war from Allah and His Messenger." — Quran 2:279

The prohibition of riba is among the most severe warnings in the Quran — framed as a declaration of war from Allah and His Messenger ﷺ.

---

## Hadith

> "The Messenger of Allah ﷺ cursed the one who consumes riba, the one who pays it, the one who writes it down, and the two who witness it, and he said: they are all the same."
> — Sahih Muslim 1598, Sunan Abu Dawud 3333, Tirmidhi 1206

This hadith explicitly includes: those who **write the contract**, those who **witness it**, and those who **facilitate** it — not only those who directly receive interest.

## Scholarly Positions on Working in Conventional Banking

### Position 1: Haram — Majority & Cautious (RECOMMENDED)

**Who says this:** Ibn Uthaymeen, Ibn Baz, IslamQA (Sheikh Al-Munajjid), AAOIFI, Islamic Fiqh Academy, Darul Uloom Deoband, virtually all classical scholars

**Ruling:** Working in a conventional bank is haram, regardless of your specific department, because:
1. The hadith explicitly condemns those who write/record/witness riba transactions
2. Even in IT, HR, or marketing, you are sustaining and enabling the riba institution
3. The principle of "no cooperation in sin and transgression" (Quran 5:2) applies
4. Your salary is paid from haram income

**References:**
- IslamQA fatwa 22905: https://islamqa.info/en/answers/22905
- IslamQA fatwa 30842 (bank employee): https://islamqa.info/en/answers/30842
- Ibn Uthaymeen: Majmoo' Fataawa, vol. 14
- Darul Uloom Deoband fatwa on bank employment

---

### Position 2: Permissible If Role Is Unrelated to Riba (Minority/Lenient)

**Who says this:** Dr. Yusuf Al-Qaradawi (with conditions), some contemporary scholars in necessity contexts

**Ruling:** If a Muslim works in IT, maintenance, security, or another function completely unrelated to riba transactions, and has no viable halal alternative (darurah/necessity), some scholars permit it.

**Conditions stated by Al-Qaradawi:**
1. The role must have zero involvement in riba contracts, accounts, or products
2. Must actively seek halal employment and leave as soon as found
3. This is a necessity (darurah) position only — not a comfortable choice

**Critique of this position:** Most mainstream scholars reject this because (a) salary still comes from haram earnings, and (b) the hadith's language covers all who sustain the riba institution, not just those directly handling interest accounts.

## Verdict for Evaluation

| Scenario | Ruling | Confidence |
|----------|--------|------------|
| Building riba loan/interest products | ❌ Haram | 100% |
| Working in IT for a riba bank (general infrastructure) | ❌ Haram (majority) / 🟠 Mukhtalaf (minority) | 95% |
| Working in HR/admin for a riba bank | ❌ Haram (majority) | 90% |
| Working for Islamic banking / takaful division within a conventional bank | 🟡 Mashbooh | 60% |
| Working for a fully Islamic bank (no riba products) | ✅ Halal | 95% |

## Practical Guidance for Job Evaluation

When evaluating a job at a financial company, ask:
1. Is the core business model based on interest/riba? → If yes: ❌ Haram (majority position)
2. What specifically would you be building? → If directly the riba product: ❌ absolutely haram
3. Is there a genuinely halal alternative available? → If yes: ❌ cannot claim necessity
4. Is the company explicitly Islamic finance / takaful? → 🟡 Evaluate separately
