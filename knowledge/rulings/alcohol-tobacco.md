# Islamic Ruling: Alcohol, Tobacco & Related Industries

**Last reviewed:** 2026-06-16
**Consensus level:** Alcohol — near-unanimous haram. Tobacco — strong majority haram.

---

## Alcohol

### Quranic Prohibition

> "O you who believe! Intoxicants, gambling, [sacrificing on] stone altars, and divining arrows are but defilement from the work of Satan, so avoid it that you may be successful." — Quran 5:90

### Hadith — The 10 Who Are Cursed

> "Allah's curse falls on ten groups of people who deal with alcohol: the one who distills it, the one for whom it has been distilled, the one who drinks it, the one who transports it, the one to whom it has been brought, the one who serves it, the one who sells it, the one who utilizes money from it, the one who buys it and the one who buys it for someone else."
> — Sunan Ibn Majah 3381, Tirmidhi 1295 (hasan)

This hadith covers the **entire supply chain** — producer, transporter, seller, and anyone who **benefits financially** from it.

### Working at an Alcohol Company — Ruling

**Consensus:** Haram. No scholarly disagreement of consequence.

- Building software for a brewery, winery, distillery, or alcohol distributor: ❌ Haram
- Working in any capacity for a company whose primary product is alcohol: ❌ Haram
- Working at a restaurant that serves alcohol: depends on role
  - If serving alcohol directly: ❌ Haram
  - If in kitchen/unrelated: 🟡 Mashbooh (some scholars permit if you never touch or serve alcohol)

**References:**
- IslamQA fatwa 1814: https://islamqa.info/en/answers/1814
- IslamQA on working at a place that sells alcohol: https://islamqa.info/en/answers/47705

## Tobacco

### Ruling

**Strong majority: Haram.** Modern scholarly consensus (20th–21st century) based on:
1. The principle of "no harm to self" (la darar wa la dirar — hadith, Ibn Majah)
2. Medical proof of severe harm to the user and others
3. Financial waste (israf) on something harmful

**Who says this:** Ibn Baz, Ibn Uthaymeen, Al-Qaradawi, IslamQA, Darul Uloom Deoband, most contemporary scholars
**Minority:** Some older scholars said makruh (disliked) rather than haram, but this view is rarely defended today given medical evidence.

### Working at a Tobacco Company — Ruling

**Majority:** Haram (same logic as alcohol — facilitating confirmed harm)
- Building software for tobacco manufacturer/distributor: ❌ Haram (majority)
- Working for tobacco IT/marketing: ❌ Haram (majority)

**References:**
- IslamQA fatwa 10922: https://islamqa.info/en/answers/10922
- Fatwa of Permanent Committee for Scholarly Research and Ifta' (Saudi Arabia) — tobacco is haram
