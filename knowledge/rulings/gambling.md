# Islamic Ruling: Gambling, Betting & Speculative Platforms

**Last reviewed:** 2026-06-16
**Consensus level:** Near-unanimous haram

---

## Quranic Prohibition

> "O you who believe! Intoxicants, gambling (maysir), [sacrificing on] stone altars, and divining arrows are but defilement from the work of Satan, so avoid it that you may be successful." — Quran 5:90

Gambling (maysir) is grouped with alcohol in a single ayah — same level of prohibition.

---

## What Counts as Gambling (Maysir)

Maysir is defined as: obtaining wealth by chance at the expense of others, where one wins only because another loses.

**Clearly haram:**
- Online casinos, poker platforms
- Sports betting / fixed-odds betting
- Lottery and scratch cards
- Slot machine manufacturers / software
- Fantasy sports with money prizes (majority view — some scholars debate if skill-based)

**Disputed:**
- Day trading with extreme speculation (some scholars: maysir; others: permissible with conditions)
- Certain video game loot boxes with real-money value (emerging scholarly discussion)
- Some forms of crypto speculation (separate evaluation)

---

## Working in Gambling Industry — Ruling

**Consensus:** Haram. The same principle as alcohol applies — facilitating maysir is maysir.

- Building casino games, betting platforms, lottery systems: ❌ Haram
- Working in IT/marketing for a gambling company: ❌ Haram (majority)
- Building payment processing specifically for gambling: ❌ Haram

**References:**
- IslamQA fatwa 3294: https://islamqa.info/en/answers/3294
- IslamQA on working for gambling company: https://islamqa.info/en/answers/22905 (same principle as riba)

## Crypto / NFTs / DeFi

This is an emerging area with significant scholarly disagreement:

| Product | Ruling | Notes |
|---------|--------|-------|
| Spot buying/selling halal-use crypto (BTC, ETH) | 🟠 Mukhtalaf | Significant scholarly disagreement; AAOIFI working on standards |
| Crypto derivatives, futures, options | ❌ Haram | Contains gharar, speculation, riba elements |
| NFT speculation / flipping | 🟠 Mukhtalaf | If underlying asset is halal, some permit; pure speculation = maysir |
| DeFi lending with interest | ❌ Haram | Riba in new form |
| Building a crypto exchange (spot only) | 🟠 Mukhtalaf | Depends on products offered |
| Building a crypto derivatives platform | ❌ Haram | Facilitates haram products |

**Recommended:** Treat crypto employment as mashbooh unless the product is clearly spot-only with no leverage/interest components.
