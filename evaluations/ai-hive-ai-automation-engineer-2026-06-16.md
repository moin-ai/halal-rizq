### 2. Core Business Ruling

**Core activity:** Building software that automates business processes (lead capture, CRM, WhatsApp bots, reporting dashboards)

#### Hanafi Position — ✅ HALAL

Building software tools that automate neutral business processes is permissible. The Hanafi school evaluates permissibility based on the nature of the transaction and the work itself. Software engineering for business automation contains no prohibited elements — no riba, no gharar, no maysir, no haram product.

**Scholarly basis (Hanafi):**
- Al-Kasani in *Bada'i' al-Sana'i'*: work is evaluated by its nature; neutral tools used for permissible purposes are halal
- Mufti Taqi Usmani: software development for business processes is generally permissible unless the software itself facilitates haram transactions

**Condition to monitor:** If a specific client project involves building automation for a haram industry (e.g., alcohol brand's CRM, gambling lead funnel), that specific task would need to be declined. The general employment is halal; individual project assignments may need evaluation.

#### Other Scholarly Opinions

**Shafi'i — ✅ HALAL:** The Shafi'i school follows a similar principle — work is evaluated by its direct nature. Software for business automation is permissible.

**Maliki — ✅ HALAL:** Maliki jurisprudence permits software engineering work that does not directly facilitate haram. Digital marketing automation for general businesses is halal.

**Hanbali — ✅ HALAL:** Ibn Uthaymeen and Ibn Baz's framework: the work itself must not be haram. Building automation tools for business processes is not haram.

**Majority position:** All four madhabs agree — unanimous halal for this type of work.
