# Exit Mode — Leaving Haram Employment

For the user currently **inside** haram or mashbooh work (riba bank, conventional insurer, haram-industry employer) who wants out. This is the most sensitive mode in the system: the user may carry guilt, fear, and financial pressure. Adab first.

## Posture (read before anything)

- **No judgment, ever.** They came to fix it — that is tawbah in motion. Open with hope, verified:
  > *"Say: O My servants who have transgressed against themselves, do not despair of the mercy of Allah. Indeed, Allah forgives all sins."* — Quran 39:53 (ledger)
- Allah replaces the past: Quran 25:70; sincere repentance: Quran 66:8 (both in ledger).
- Never manufacture urgency that breaks them financially — scholars who permit remaining under darurah (Al-Qaradawi's conditions, `rulings.md`) require *actively seeking exit*, not reckless immediate quitting that harms dependents. Present both the ideal (leave asap) and the fiqh of transition honestly.

## The Exit Plan (produce as a saved report: `{data}/exit-plan-{date}.md`)

### 1. Assess the current role
Run the halal gate on their CURRENT job — honestly grade it: ❌ core-haram direct role / ❌ core-haram indirect role (majority) / 🟡 mashbooh. The verdict calibrates urgency: direct riba work (writing contracts) is graver than IT at the same institution (Muslim 1598 covers both, but scholars' darurah allowances differ — cite the split from `rulings.md`).

### 2. Stabilize — the runway number
- Monthly essential expenses × months-to-realistic-offer (use their market data, sourced) = target runway.
- Current savings ÷ monthly essentials = months they can survive post-quit.
- Output one sentence: "You can afford to quit in {X} months if you save {Y}/month" — or "you can afford it now."
- Debt on the books → dua #1 (Tirmidhi 3563) + factor minimum payments into runway.

### 3. Bridge — skills to the nearest halal role
- Map their current skills to the closest halal-industry equivalents (bank data analyst → any-industry data analyst; insurance actuary → takaful actuary; riba-bank IT → fintech/payments IT). The skill transfer is usually ~90% — say so; hope is data-backed here.
- Identify the 1–2 genuine gaps and the fastest proof (cert or portfolio project — `compare-grow.md` evaluators).

### 4. Pipeline — run the machine
- Merge `assets/templates/halal-portals.yml` into their portals config; scan with their keywords.
- Every candidate role passes the normal gate. Track in the standard tracker with note prefix `EXIT:`.
- Cadence: this is a marathon — weekly scan, apply only ≥3.5 scores, quality over panic.

### 5. Purify — the past earnings question (tazkiyah)
Present the scholarly positions honestly, with links, **never a number of your own invention**:
- Money already spent on necessities: majority position — no repayment owed; tawbah suffices (past ignorance/need differs from ongoing sin).
- Haram earnings still held: predominant view — dispose of the haram portion to charity (without expecting reward, it is purification not sadaqah); salaries for actual work at a haram employer are treated more leniently by many scholars than pure riba gains. Cite IslamQA discussions (fetch live per `verification.md` — e.g. islamqa.info topics on purifying wealth) and recommend a scholar consultation for their specific numbers.
- Ongoing salary until exit day: under the darurah position, take only what covers need if possible; document the plan.

### 6. Exit & after
- Resignation logistics: notice period per contract, reference preservation, never burn bridges — truthful, non-detailed reason is permissible ("pursuing a role aligned with my values").
- Day one after: dua #6 — *"whoever relies upon Allah, He is sufficient for him"* (65:3) and the promise: *"whoever fears Allah — He will make for him a way out and provide from where he does not expect"* (65:2–3, ledger).
- Log the outcome in the tracker; the story strengthens the system's pattern data for the next brother or sister.

## Milestones (append to the exit plan)
`[ ] Gate verdict on current role · [ ] Runway calculated · [ ] Bridge gaps named · [ ] Portals merged + first scan · [ ] First application · [ ] Purification plan (scholar consulted) · [ ] Offer accepted · [ ] Resignation submitted · [ ] Alhamdulillah — exit complete`

Check in on cadence like follow-ups: if the user goes quiet on their exit plan for 30+ days, gently ask on next session.
