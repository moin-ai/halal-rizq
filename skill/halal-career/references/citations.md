## Numbering-mismatch protocol

When a lookup returns a different hadith than expected:
1. Do not discard — check the collection's numbering schemes (in-book vs. global vs. Abd al-Baqi vs. Darussalam).
2. Locate the intended hadith by text search in the same collection.
3. Cite BOTH numbers: "Muslim 1598 (Abd al-Baqi) / CDN#4093".
4. Add the mapping to this ledger.
