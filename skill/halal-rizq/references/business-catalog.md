# Business Model Catalog — Fiqh-Axis Mapping

Pre-mapped starting points for the Business Gate (`business-gate.md`). **A catalog lean is NOT a verdict** — the gate's intake still runs, because specifics flip outcomes (a "halal-lean" model funded by an interest loan is haram; a "haram-lean" niche restructured can clear). Rulings and citations live in `rulings.md` / `citations.md`; this file only maps models onto axes.

## The axes (closed set — every business decomposes onto these)

| Key | Axis | Rulings section |
|-----|------|-----------------|
| RIBA | Interest in funding, streams, or product | § Riba; § Funding |
| GHARAR | Contract uncertainty | § Gharar & maysir |
| MAYSIR | Chance-based gain | § Gambling; § Gharar & maysir |
| PRODUCT | The thing sold is itself haram | § Alcohol/Tobacco; § Gambling; etc. |
| QABD | Selling without ownership/possession | § Selling without possession |
| GHISH | Deception in product or marketing | § Marketing honesty |
| ASSIST | Assisting sin — serving haram businesses (Quran 5:2) | § Indirect work; § Oppression |
| GENDER | Khalwa, ikhtilat, awrah, tabarruj | § Gender Interaction |
| MUSIC | Instruments/entertainment khilaf | § Music & Entertainment |
| BRIBE | Corruption-dependent operations | § Bribery |
| LABOR | Wage abuse, haram tasks forced on workers | § Worker Rights |
| COMPOUND | Two-sales-in-one, hidden financing | § Compound Contracts |
| OPPRESS | Watchlist complicity | § Oppression Complicity |
| HARM | Products harming users (tobacco principle) | § Alcohol/Tobacco (harm) |

**Future-proofing rule:** any business not listed here — including models that don't exist yet — is handled by running the intake and mapping its components onto these axes. New models never need new fiqh axes; they need honest decomposition. After ruling on an unlisted model, ADD it to this catalog (self-growing, like the employer index).

## Using this catalog

1. Gate intake first, always. 2. Look the model up here → load the listed rulings sections. 3. The "what decides it" column tells you which intake answers matter most. 4. Verdict per `business-gate.md` Step 4 — CONDITIONAL with named fixes is the expected common outcome. 5. Unlisted model → decompose onto the axes, rule, then append a row here (keep this file under 500 lines; split by sector if it grows past that).
