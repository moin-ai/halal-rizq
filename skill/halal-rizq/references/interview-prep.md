# Interview Prep — Sourced Intel + Deen Readiness

Trigger: user says "prep me for the {company} interview" or tracker status becomes `Interview`.

## Sourcing rules (same discipline as everything else)
- Every reported question, stat, or process detail carries a source link or the tag `[inferred from JD]`. Never attribute an invented question to Glassdoor/Blind. Data missing → "unknown — not enough data", never a guess.

## 1. Research
WebSearch, extract structured facts with sources:
- `{company} {role} interview questions site:glassdoor.com` → real questions, rounds, difficulty, timeline
- `{company} interview process site:teamblind.com` → candid process/negotiation details
- `{company} engineering blog` → stack, values, vocabulary they use internally
- `{company} interview process {role}` → gap-filler (write-ups, videos)
Sparse results (small company) → say so; prep from the JD + archetype instead, labeled as inferred.

## 2. Output (save to `{data}/interview/{company}-{role}.md`)
- **Process overview:** rounds, format, duration, difficulty, quirks — sourced.
- **Round-by-round:** what each evaluates, reported questions (sourced), 1–2 prep actions each.
- **Likely questions:** technical / behavioral / role-specific / background red-flags (gaps, transitions the interviewer will probe — with honest, forward-looking framings). For each: the candidate's best angle, citing CV lines.
- **Story bank mapping:** table of likely topics → best story from `{data}/interview/story-bank.md` → fit (strong/partial/none). For each `none`: name the CV experience that could become the missing story, offer to draft it STAR+R and append to the bank.
- **Company signals:** values they screen for (sourced), internal vocabulary to use, anti-patterns to avoid, 2–3 sharp questions to ask them tied to recent company news.

## 3. Deen Readiness (unique to this skill)
Prepare the candidate to assess and negotiate religious accommodation — factually, without apology:

**To find out (ask HR/manager at the right moment, usually post-offer or final round):**
- Prayer: space for salah, flexibility for prayer times; Jumu'ah (Friday ~1hr, varies by location) arrangement
- Ramadan: schedule flexibility, iftar-time considerations for on-site/shift roles
- Food/drink: halal options at team events; norms around alcohol-centered socials — and the ease of opting out
- Leave: Eid days (annual-leave planning if not public holidays)

**Framing scripts (adapt to user's voice):**
- "I pray briefly a few times a day — it takes a few minutes and has never affected my delivery. Is there a quiet space colleagues use?"
- "On Fridays I attend a congregational prayer around midday — I make the time up. Is that workable for this team?"
- Timing advice: disclose needs confidently but strategically — accommodation questions are strongest **after** they want you (offer stage), except hard constraints the user prefers to state upfront.

**Rights context (jurisdiction-specific — verify per country, cite what's found):** WebSearch `religious accommodation workplace law {country}` — e.g. reasonable-accommodation duties (US Title VII, UK Equality Act 2010, EU directive 2000/78/EC); Malaysia: Federal Constitution Art. 11 context. Present findings with links, labeled as information, not legal advice.

**If the environment is hostile to basic practice** → that is Stage 1 information: note it in the company dossier and the report's Annotations.
