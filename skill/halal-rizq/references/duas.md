# Duas for the Jobseeker — Verified

Arabic fetched verbatim from source APIs 2026-06-16 (quran.com; fawazahmed0 CDN ara- editions). Translations: Saheeh International (Quran) / collection translations (hadith). Do not modify Arabic text.

## 1. Sufficiency through halal — THE jobseeker dua
**When:** seeking halal income, financial hardship, debt — the Prophet ﷺ said it would suffice "even a debt like Mount Sir".

> اللَّهُمَّ اكْفِنِي بِحَلاَلِكَ عَنْ حَرَامِكَ وَأَغْنِنِي بِفَضْلِكَ عَمَّنْ سِوَاكَ

*Allahumma-kfini bi-halalika 'an haramika, wa aghnini bi-fadlika 'amman siwak*
"O Allah, suffice me with what You have made lawful instead of what You have made unlawful, and make me independent of all others besides You."
— **Tirmidhi 3563** (Ahmad Shakir: Hasan; Al-Albani: Hasan)

## 2. Refuge from anxiety, incapacity, and debt
**When:** job-search stress, rejection, financial pressure. The Prophet ﷺ said it regularly.

> اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنَ الْهَمِّ وَالْحَزَنِ، وَالْعَجْزِ وَالْكَسَلِ، وَالْجُبْنِ وَالْبُخْلِ، وَضَلَعِ الدَّيْنِ، وَغَلَبَةِ الرِّجَالِ

*Allahumma inni a'udhu bika minal-hammi wal-hazan, wal-'ajzi wal-kasal, wal-jubni wal-bukhl, wa dala'id-dayni wa ghalabatir-rijal*
"O Allah, I seek refuge with You from worry and grief, from incapacity and laziness, from cowardice and miserliness, from being heavily in debt and from being overpowered by men."
— **Bukhari 6369**

## 3. Morning dua for beneficial knowledge and good provision
**When:** after Fajr — the Prophet ﷺ said it upon finishing the morning prayer.

> اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسْأَلُكَ عِلْمًا نَافِعًا، وَرِزْقًا طَيِّبًا، وَعَمَلاً مُتَقَبَّلاً

*Allahumma inni as'aluka 'ilman nafi'an, wa rizqan tayyiban, wa 'amalan mutaqabbalan*
"O Allah, I ask You for knowledge that is beneficial, provision that is good, and deeds that are accepted."
— **Ibn Majah 925** (Al-Albani: Sahih; Abd al-Baqi: Sahih)

## 4. Musa's dua when destitute — before any door opened
**When:** unemployed, between opportunities. Musa عليه السلام said it — and provision came the same day.

> رَبِّ إِنِّى لِمَآ أَنزَلْتَ إِلَىَّ مِنْ خَيْرٍ فَقِيرٌ

*Rabbi inni lima anzalta ilayya min khayrin faqir*
"My Lord, indeed I am, for whatever good You would send down to me, in need."
— **Quran 28:24**

## 5. Before interviews and presentations — Musa's dua before facing Pharaoh

> قَالَ رَبِّ ٱشْرَحْ لِى صَدْرِى

*Rabbi-shrah li sadri* (continue 20:26–28: *wa yassir li amri, wahlul 'uqdatan min lisani, yafqahu qawli*)
"My Lord, expand for me my breast [with assurance], ease for me my task, untie the knot from my tongue, that they may understand my speech."
— **Quran 20:25–28**

## 6. Tawakkul — provision from where you do not expect
**When:** a door closes; a haram opportunity is declined for Allah's sake.

> وَيَرْزُقْهُ مِنْ حَيْثُ لَا يَحْتَسِبُ ۚ وَمَن يَتَوَكَّلْ عَلَى ٱللَّهِ فَهُوَ حَسْبُهُۥٓ

"...And He will provide for him from where he does not expect. And whoever relies upon Allah — then He is sufficient for him."
— **Quran 65:3** (with 65:2: "whoever fears Allah — He will make for him a way out")

## 7. Istikharah — when the verdict is doubtful
**When:** MASHBOOH/MUKHTALAF verdicts, competing offers, any major decision.
Jabir رضي الله عنه: "The Prophet ﷺ used to teach us Istikharah in all matters as he used to teach us a Surah of the Quran." — **Bukhari 1166**. Pray 2 rak'ahs, then the full Istikharah dua (text at sunnah.com/bukhari:1166).

## Usage in reports

- HARAM verdict → dua 6 (a door closed for Allah's sake) or dua 1.
- MASHBOOH/MUKHTALAF → dua 7 (Istikharah), optionally dua 1.
- HALAL + applying → dua 3 or 5 (interview stage).
- Rejection/hardship context → dua 2 or 4.
One dua per report; match the moment. Quote Arabic + transliteration + translation + source exactly as above.
