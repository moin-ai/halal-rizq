# Outreach & Follow-up

## LinkedIn Outreach
Find targets via WebSearch (`site:linkedin.com/in {company} recruiter|{team} manager|{role}`). Never fabricate a person — only message profiles actually found, cite the profile URL to the user.

**Message = 3 sentences, ≤300 chars** (connection-request limit). Emphasis shifts by contact type:

| Target | S1 | S2 | S3 |
|--------|----|----|----|
| Recruiter | direct fit: role + top criteria met | pre-answer their screen (years, location, availability) | "Happy to share my CV if this aligns" |
| Hiring manager | the team's specific challenge (from JD/dossier) | your quantified result solving something similar | ask about their approach to that challenge |
| Peer | genuine reference to their work (post, talk, repo) | what you're building in the same space — NOT a job pitch | invite their take; referral emerges naturally or not at all |
| Interviewer (scheduled) | specific reference to their work | light connection to your experience | "Looking forward to our conversation on {date}" |

**Honesty rules:** the "genuine interest" must be genuine — never feign familiarity with work you haven't looked at (have their post/repo actually fetched before referencing it). No flattery scripts. Never share phone numbers. No cold-pitching a peer for a referral.

## Follow-up Cadence

| Status | First | Then | Cap |
|--------|-------|------|-----|
| Applied | +7 days | every 7 days | 2 total → mark cold |
| Responded | +1 day (urgent) | every 3 days | — |
| Interview done | +1 day (thank-you) | every 3 days | — |

Track in `{data}/follow-ups.md` (`| # | company | role | sent date | channel | note |`). Record only what the user confirms was actually sent.

**First follow-up email** (≤150 words + subject): reference role + application date → one concrete value-add from the evaluation's proof points → soft ask with a specific window. Banned: "just checking in", "just following up", "touching base", "circling back". Lead with value, not the ask.
**Second:** shorter, new angle (relevant insight or project update) — never repeat the first.
**Cold (2 sent, no reply):** stop. Suggest: different contact, or status → `Discarded`, or deprioritize.

When asked "what's due?": scan the tracker's Applied/Responded/Interview rows against the cadence table, list overdue items sorted by urgency, and draft only those.
