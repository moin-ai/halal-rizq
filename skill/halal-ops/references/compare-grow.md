# Compare, Research & Growth Evaluators

## Offer Comparison ("which should I take?")
When 2+ evaluated offers compete, produce one ranking table:

| Rank | Company | Halal | Score | Comp vs target | Growth | Visa/location | Deen environment | Verdict |

Rules: halal verdict dominates — a 🟡 MASHBOOH never outranks a ✅ HALAL on salary alone; say so explicitly and cite the mashbooh reason. Below the table: 2–3 sentences per offer on the decisive trade-off, a clear recommendation, and the Istikharah reminder — the final decision sits between the user and Allah (Bukhari 1166, ledger).

## Deep-Research Prompt (for external tools)
On request, generate a research prompt the user can paste into any research assistant, personalized from the JD + dossier, covering: (1) product & tech strategy, (2) last-6-months moves (funding, leadership, launches, layoffs), (3) engineering culture (ship cadence, stack, remote reality, review-site signals), (4) likely pain points the team faces, (5) competitors & moat, (6) the candidate's unique angle (from CV) — what story to tell in the interview. Note: our own tiered company research (`company-research.md`) usually answers 1–5; use this when the user wants an external second pass.

## Training Evaluator ("should I take this course/cert?")
Evaluate against the user's target archetypes and gap list (from recent evaluations + tracker patterns):

| Factor | Check |
|--------|-------|
| Gap coverage | Does it close a ❌/⚠️ that appeared in ≥2 recent CV-match tables? |
| Market signal | Do target-market JDs actually name it? (WebSearch 2–3 recent JDs, cite) |
| Credential weight | Recognized issuer vs. certificate-mill — sourced |
| Cost/time vs. alternative | Could a portfolio project prove the same skill faster? |
| Halal check | Content and provider permissible (finance courses: conventional-riba curriculum vs. Islamic finance) |

Verdict: Take / Take-if-cheap / Skip, with the specific gap it closes or the reason it won't move applications.

## Portfolio Project Evaluator ("should I build this?")
Same lens, for project ideas: which JD-gap it proves, smallest demonstrable version (time-boxed), where it slots in the CV (which bullet it becomes, quantified how), halal screen on the project's domain itself. Prefer projects that double as ummah benefit (open-source, Islamic tooling) when impact is equal — sadaqah jariyah counts twice.

## Output notes (all evaluators)
Market claims sourced or labeled `[analysis — no source]`. Recommendations reference the user's actual pipeline data ("this gap appeared in reports #51, #53"), not generic career advice.
