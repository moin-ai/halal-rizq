# Stage 2 — Career Fit Framework

Runs only when Stage 1 verdict ≠ HARAM. Candidate data: `config/profile.yml` → `cv_path`/`cv_data` (or ask user for CV if absent).

## A) Role Summary
Table: Archetype (vs. candidate's primary/secondary) · Domain · Seniority · Location/model · one-line TL;DR of what the role actually buys.

## B) CV Match
Table per key JD requirement: | JD Requirement | CV Evidence | ✅ Strong / ⚠️ Partial / ❌ Gap |. After the table: which gaps are hard blockers vs. bridgeable, and how.

## C) Level & Strategy
Seniority alignment; how to frame experience to clear the hardest gate; the narrative anchor for this application.

## D) Comp & Demand
Market range (location+level) vs. candidate target vs. likely offer; name the gap.
**Sourcing required:** salary figures come from WebSearch of salary sites for that market (Glassdoor, Payscale, levels.fyi, JobStreet/Jora for SEA, StepStone for DE) — cite the link and retrieval date. No data found → give the range labeled `[estimate — no market data found]`. Never present an unsourced number as market fact.

## E) Personalization Plan
Numbered table: Section → Change → Why (mirror JD vocabulary; reorder bullets; surface matching skills).

## F) Interview Prep — STAR+R
Top 4–5 JD requirements → | Requirement | Story | Result (quantified) | Reflection |. Then anticipated hard questions with honest model answers (address gaps directly, never fabricate experience).

## G) Posting Legitimacy
| Signal | Finding | Weight |: JD specificity, company verifiable, salary disclosed, posting age, visa/work rights. Assessment: High/Medium/Low confidence.

## Score (/5)
Primary: CV match strength. Deductions (state each): −0.5 unconfirmed visa sponsorship · −0.3 significant domain gap · −0.5 seniority mismatch · −0.3 comp below candidate floor.
4.5–5 Strong Apply · 3.5–4.4 Apply · 2.5–3.4 Borderline · <2.5 Skip.
